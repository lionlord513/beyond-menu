# 0.2.2

- Preserve paintball splashes, wall drips and ground pools across saves and game restarts. Loading an older slot in the same world keeps its paint and cleanup history.
- Tap middle mouse to put away the equipped paintball gun, or hold it and drag to rotate.
- Scrub paint with the native polishing brush to remove it. Saving after cleanup keeps the scrubbed area clean when you reload.

# 0.2.1

- Keep paintball gun input from also activating the world object or car under the crosshair.
- Keep the loaded paintball count visible while using the gun.
- Vary the impact shapes and make repeated hits produce visible wall drips and growing ground pools.

# 0.2.0

- Spawn a paintball marker and 50-round canisters in the new Fun tab. Choose the fill with the color wheel. Aim at a canister with the marker in hand and press **L** to top up matching paint or swap to a different color.
- Paintballs leave colored splashes. Repeated hits on wet paint form runs on walls and pools on floors. The mod stores paint and ammunition alongside each native save revision.
- Show paintballs in flight using a pool of reusable projectile meshes. Limit surface traces while drawing permanent splashes.
- Toggle flight and noclip from Player, hold Shift to accelerate, or select Open Computer to access the in-game desktop.
- Compact the paintball controls and simplify menu text.

# 0.1.1

- Fix BMM ZIP import, declare the tested UE4SS build, and initialize thumbnail storage before opening the menu.

# 0.1.0

- First standalone BeyondMenu release, opened with F8.
- New rounded, transparent interface and saved color settings.
- Native item data, icons, search, custom spraypaint and unlimited supplies.
- Stock and tuned vehicle pictures, delayed hover preview, a car color wheel with metallic/matte finishes and native car spawning.
- Bright vehicle thumbnails with complete stock/tuned paint bases. Visible icons update first in the background without rebuilding the page or clearing search.
- Host scoreboard actions, including the native owning-client teleport route.
- Optional item providers for separately installed Beyond Apocalypse and Beyond Tuning; Hornet, motorcycles and trailer appear only in the car spawner.
- GitHub update metadata and a standalone checksum-verified updater.
